# Masirah (مسيرة)

AI-powered, bilingual (Arabic/English) CV screening and applicant tracking platform for the Egyptian and wider MENA hiring market.

## Language

**Masirah (مسيرة)**:
The product and brand name. The domain `masirah.online` is owned.
_Avoid_: Siirah, Seerah — the draft working name used in early versions of the BRD, now superseded.

**CV (سيرة ذاتية)**:
The document a candidate submits, in any format or language. In Arabic the bare word "سيرة" means CV generically and is never the brand — the brand is "مسيرة". Keep the two apart when editing Arabic copy.
_Avoid_: resume, profile — a `parsed_profile` is the structured extraction from a CV, not the CV itself.
