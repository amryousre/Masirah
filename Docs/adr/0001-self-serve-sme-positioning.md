# Self-serve SME positioning over full ATS competition

Three positioning options were weighed (BRD Section 5): a self-serve SME wedge (free tier, monthly EGP billing, no sales call), a screening-only layer integrating with existing ATS products, or head-on feature competition with Recruitera. We're going with the first combined with the second: Masirah wins on self-serve monthly pricing and on depth of CV screening, not on breadth of ATS features — requisition management, offer letters and manpower planning stay out of scope. Every funded competitor (Recruitera, RecDesk) sells annual contracts above the SME price anchor through a sales-led process; matching their feature breadth would demand a slower, sales-led build the solo founder isn't resourced for, while the actual incumbent (inbox + spreadsheet) is beaten by screening depth alone.
